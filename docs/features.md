# Features

[← Back to README](../README.md)

The interface is in Italian. The Italian menu labels are given in brackets.

## Views

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| 3D (`3D`) | Orbit around the whole flat |
| Floor plan (`Pianta`) | Top-down view |
| Walk-through (`Cammina`) | First-person view from inside the rooms |

`Ripristina` resets the camera for the current view.

## Controls

**3D and floor plan**

- Drag to rotate, right-click or two fingers to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom.
- Hover over a room to see its measurements.
- Click a door to open or close it.

**Walk-through**

- `W` `A` `S` `D` to move, mouse to look around, `Esc` to pause.
- On touch devices: left thumb is a joystick, right thumb looks around.
- Left-click a door to open or close it. At night, left-click a lamp to switch it on or off.
- Right-click (or the `Misura` button) to use the tape measure: set point A, then point B. It snaps to corners
  and edges.

## Light

- **Day (`Giorno`)**: a slider moves the sun through the day. In walk-through mode, daylight comes in through the
  windows and bounces around the rooms (global illumination).
- **Night (`Notte`)**: the flat is lit by its own lamps. Each lamp can be switched on and off, with a slider for
  colour temperature and a dimmer for the living room.

## Display options

Under `Generale`: labels, fixed fittings, furniture, ceilings, air conditioning and ventilation, drawing lines and
a 1 m grid can each be shown or hidden. Doors can be opened or closed all at once, and walls can be shown full
height or cut at 1.20 m.

## Furniture and finishes

Under `Stanze`, each room lists its options: for example the dining table (closed or extended), dining chairs,
sofa and bed colours, the rug, and the kitchen stone, sink and tap.

## Surface check

`Verifica superfici` compares each room's floor area in the model with the area on the architect's drawing.

## Saved choices

Furniture, colours, light settings and open menu sections are remembered in the browser, per device.
